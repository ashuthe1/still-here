This is from a man who got all of it. The money, the fame, the face on the side of the bus, every single thing he wrote down as a kid.

He is not telling you not to want things. He is sending a postcard from the far side of getting them, and the postcard says: it wasn't here.

Which is either the worst news you have had all year or the best. Because if the answer was not at the top, then it is not waiting for you up there either. Whatever it is, it is available now, on your current salary, in your current flat, with your current haircut.
