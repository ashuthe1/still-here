@note
In ninth grade, I failed an English essay. Red ink everywhere.
---
@note
At the bottom, one sentence was underlined twice. In the margin:

==This sentence is yours. Write more like it.==
---
@glasses
I didn't understand. I read that sentence twenty times.

It was about my grandmother's hands.
---
@book
The next essay, I tried to write more like it. C minus.

The one after that, a B.
---
@chairs
Mrs D'Souza almost never smiled. Once she said:

> Stop writing what you think I want. I've read that already.
---
@note
Now I write for a living.

~ Mostly instructions for washing machines. But still.
---
@envelope
Last year I found her address and sent her a letter. Two pages, about that sentence.
---
@envelope
Three weeks later, a reply. My own letter, sent back.

Red ink in the margin:

> This one is yours too.
