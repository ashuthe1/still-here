There's a corner shop near the hospital. Mr Okafor runs it.

I never once read the sign on the door.
---
@bus
I worked nights that year. My shift started at eight.

I'd run in at 7:40, out of breath, for milk and something sweet.
---
@mug
He was always there, lights on.

> Busy day?
< Not yet.
---
@clock
Three years of that. Rain, power cuts, the night of the big match.

Lights on at 7:40.
---
@bus
One evening my bus broke down. I got there at five past eight, sure I'd missed him.

The lights were on.
---
@shop
He was behind the counter with his coat already on, reading the paper.

> There you are.
---
@door
That's when I finally read the sign.

==Monday to Saturday, 7am to 7pm.==
---
@clock
< Mr Okafor. You close at seven.
> You come at twenty to eight.
---
@shop
# Three years.
He had kept the shop open late every night, for one carton of milk.
---
@mug
The next night I brought him a coffee.

> Too sweet.

He drank all of it.

~ The sign still says seven. The lights still go off at eight.
