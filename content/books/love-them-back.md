# Call for no reason.
Not on a birthday. On a Tuesday, to ask what they had for lunch.
---
@phone
# Teach them the phone, slowly.
The third time too. They taught you to use a spoon.
---
@rotis
# Eat what they cook, and say so.
"This is good" costs nothing and lasts all week.
---
@chai
# Ask about their youth.
Who were they at twenty? You'll meet a stranger you'll like.
---
@envelope
# Let them pay sometimes.
It isn't about the money. It's about still being able to look after you.
---
@glasses
# Take photos of them, not just with them.
One day you'll want their face more than yours.
---
@note
# Tell them the boring parts.
What you ate, what your boss said. They want the boring parts most.
---
@bus
# Visit.
More often than feels necessary.
