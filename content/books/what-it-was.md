A: The man who left the party after twenty minutes.
B: He had promised his daughter he'd be home for the end of her film.
---
A: The woman on her phone all through the wedding.
B: Her mother was in hospital. Every message said "still stable".
---
A: The boy who never takes his hood down in class.
B: His mum cut his hair last night. It went badly. He'll be fine by Friday.
---
A: The neighbour who never says hello.
B: She works nights. For her it's two in the morning.
---
A: The colleague who's always rushing out at five.
B: The nursery closes at half past. There is no plan B.
---
A: The friend who went quiet for months.
B: She was getting through something, one week at a time. She's back now. Say hello.
---
A: The old man counting coins slowly at the till.
B: He ran a shop for forty years. He likes taking his time in one.
---
A: The man crying at the bus stop.
B: He'd just been offered the job. Six months of trying.
---
A: The person who snapped at you today.
B: Also having a hard day. Possibly the same one.
