# Find the warm spot.
It moves around the house as the day goes on.

Follow it. That's the whole plan.
---
# Rest before you're tired.
I slept at nine, at eleven, and at two.

Nobody asked what I'd achieved.
---
# Stretch first.
Before getting up. Before lying down. Before anything.

Every single time.
---
# Watch without joining in.
There's a bird outside. I've been watching it for an hour.

I have no plans regarding the bird.
---
# Say no clearly.
When I don't want to be picked up, I make it known.

Everyone understands. Nobody is offended for long.
---
# Ask for what you want.
I stand by the bowl and look at it. Then at you. Then at the bowl.

==It works.==
---
# Sit on the important papers.
If they're spread across the whole table, it was probably time for a break.
---
# Disappear for a while.
Some afternoons I'm simply not available.

Then I'm back, and nobody minds.
---
# Purr when you're happy.
Let people know when something is good.

It costs nothing, and they remember.
