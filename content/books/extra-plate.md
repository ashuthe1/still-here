My first year in the city, I lived alone in a room above a tailor's shop.

Every Friday at eight, there was a knock.
---
@tiffin
Mrs Iyer from downstairs, holding a steel tiffin.

> Made too much. Don't waste it.
---
@rotis
She said it every Friday for two years.

She never once made too much on a Thursday.
---
@envelope
I tried to pay her once.

> Then next week I'll make less.
---
@phone
I never found out how she knew I was living on instant noodles.

~ I think the dustbin told her.

Then I got a job in another city.

On my last Friday, the tiffin had a note on top: ==Eat proper food there also.==
