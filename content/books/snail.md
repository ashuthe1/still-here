# Wait for your weather.
I only go out after rain.

Some call it fussy. I call it knowing yourself.
---
# Leave a little trail.
Wherever I've been, there's a shiny line.

Proof I went somewhere. Even if it was just round the flowerpot.
---
# Go back in when you need to.
A leaf touched my eye this morning. I went inside for an hour.

Came back out. All fine.
---
# Everything is a journey if you're small.
Today I crossed the whole patio.

My friend was very supportive. My friend is a leaf.
---
# You don't need to see the whole path.
My eyes are on stalks and I still only see as far as the next stone.

Doesn't stop me.
---
# Carry what fits.
My house is exactly my size.

Everything I own, I can carry. It's a very light life.
---
# Slow is still arriving.
I reached the lettuce eventually. It had a bite out of it already.

==Still worth it.==
---
# Come out again tomorrow.
Bad day in the garden? Shell. Sleep. Rain.

Try again.
