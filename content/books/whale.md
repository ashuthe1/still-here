# Come up for air.
Even I can't stay down forever.

Every so often, surface. Breathe. Look at the sky.
---
# Go deep when it's loud up top.
Up there it's all waves and boats.

Down here it's dark and calm. I think better.
---
# Sing long songs.
Some of mine take twenty minutes.

Someone far away always answers.
---
# Turn slowly.
Big things change direction slowly.

Give yourself time.
---
# Travel far for the good things.
I swim thousands of kilometres for the right water.

Some things are worth the trip.
---
# Little things add up.
I'm the size of a bus and I live on tiny shrimp.

==Very small, very often.==
---
# Keep your family close.
We travel together for years.

Nobody swims the whole ocean alone.
---
# Leap sometimes.
Mostly I'm quiet. But now and then I jump right out of the water, for no reason at all.

Everyone loves it. So do I.
