# Like water.
The best way to live is like water.

It helps everything, and fights with nothing.
---
# Soft and strong.
Nothing in the world is softer than water.

And nothing is better at wearing down stone.
---
# On knowing.
Knowing other people is clever.

Knowing yourself is wise.
---
# On enough.
Whoever knows they have enough is rich.
---
# On long journeys.
A journey of a thousand miles begins with the ground under your feet.
---
# On muddy water.
Be still for a while, and the mud settles.

Then the water is clear.
---
# On showing off.
If you stand on tiptoe, you can't stand for long.

~ Chapters 8, 78, 33, 64, 15 and 24
