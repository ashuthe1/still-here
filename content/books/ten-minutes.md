@phone
# To call the person you keep meaning to call.
Most of those calls last eight minutes.

You've been putting off eight minutes for a month.
---
@laundry
# To clear one surface.
Just the one. The kitchen table, or the chair with all the clothes on it.
---
@keys
# To walk round the block.
Leave your phone. Look at other people's front doors.
---
@book
# To read a few pages.
Ten minutes a day comes to about a book a month.
---
@chai
# To sit outside with a cup of tea.
No phone, no plan.

See what the sky is doing.
---
@note
# To write down one thing from today.
In a year, that's 365 days you would otherwise have forgotten.
---
@sun
# To lie on the floor.
Really. Arms out.

Let the floor hold the weight for a bit.
---
@clock
# To start the thing you're avoiding.
Not finish it. Start it.

Ten minutes in, it is never as bad as the dread was.
---
@envelope
# To say sorry.
It takes about thirty seconds.

The other nine and a half are for working up to it.
