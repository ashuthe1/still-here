@plane
Flight delayed four hours.

The man next to me, about eighty, offered me half his sandwich.
---
@suitcase
> Where are you off to?
< A job interview. If I make it in time.
> You'll make it. Or you'll make a different one.
---
@clock
< You seem very calm about the delay.
> At my age, waiting is free time.
---
@glasses
< What would you tell yourself at thirty?
> Stop checking the departures board every two minutes. It doesn't go faster.
---
@phone
< Seriously, though.
> Seriously. And call your mother more.
> And buy good shoes. You'll walk a lot more than you think.
---
@suitcase
< Why are you flying?
> My granddaughter's dance recital. She dances for forty seconds.
> I wouldn't miss it.
---
@plane
At last they called our flight. He shook my hand as if we'd done business.

> Good luck. Wear the good shoes.
---
@sun
I got the job.

~ I still think about the sandwich.
