Every school day, my dad wrote something on the paper napkin in my lunch box.

A joke. A drawing of a dog. ==Good luck with the maths test.==

@note
In first grade I read them out loud to my whole table.

By fifth grade I read them quickly, then folded them away.

After a while, I forgot it had ever been any other way.

Inside were napkins. Hundreds of them, each one dated.
