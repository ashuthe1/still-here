# Two kinds of things.
Some things are up to you: what you think, what you want, what you do.

Most other things aren't. Know which is which.
---
# On being upset.
It's rarely the thing itself that upsets us.

It's what we tell ourselves about it.
---
# On loving a jug.
If you love a jug, remember it's a jug.

Then, if it breaks, you won't be broken with it.
---
# On looking foolish.
If you want to get better at something,

be willing to look foolish for a while.
---
# On rude people.
When someone is rude to you, remember: they're doing what seems right to them.

They're the one who's mistaken. You don't have to join them.
---
# At the dinner table.
Live as you would at a dinner party. When a dish comes round, take a polite share.

If it passes you by, don't reach across for it.
---
# On showing, not telling.
Don't explain your philosophy. Live it.

Sheep don't show the shepherd how much grass they ate. ==They make wool.==
