# Every jump starts with a crouch.
Getting smaller before a big leap is normal.

It's the getting ready part.
---
# You won't always land where you meant to.
I aimed for a lily pad and landed in a teacup someone left on the wall.

Made a new friend. A wasp. We're not close.
---
# Fell in? Swim.
Frogs are good at water.

Climb back up on the next pad.
---
# Sing at night, even off-key.
The whole pond sings after dark. Some of us are not good.

Nobody minds. It's about joining in.
---
# You were a tadpole once.
No legs. Very small plans.

Look at you now.
---
# Rest in the sun between jumps.
Not every hour is a leap.

Most of them are sitting.
---
# Let things come to you.
I don't chase flies across the pond.

I wait for the ones that come close. ==Much less tiring.==
---
# Jump anyway.
The next pad always looks far.

It's usually closer than it looks.
