A: That everyone saw the stain on my shirt.
B: One person did. She showed me hers.
---
A: That I'd said something stupid in the meeting.
B: Nobody remembers. My manager doesn't remember the meeting.
---
A: That they were angry, because they hadn't replied.
B: Their phone was in the washing machine.
---
A: That I'd never make friends in a new city.
B: It took a year. Then it took one quiz night.
---
A: That I was too old to start.
B: The oldest person in the class was sixty-one. She was the best.
---
A: That they'd say no.
B: They said, "I thought you'd never ask."
---
A: That I'd be alone at the wedding.
B: I was seated with the aunties. Best table in the room.
---
A: That I had ruined everything.
B: I had ruined dinner. We ordered pizza.
