When Mr Shah retired, he stopped going out.

He said he was fine. He watched a lot of news.
---

His daughter brought home a dog from the shelter.

> I don't want a dog.
< Just for a week, Papa. A trial.
---
@dog
The dog was called Biscuit.

She did not care about the news.
---
@clock

until he put his shoes on.

And some of their owners.

> Don't be silly. She's settled. Moving is very stressful for dogs.
---

Biscuit is very settled.
