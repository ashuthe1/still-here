# He never meant anyone to read it.
Almost two thousand years ago, the most powerful man in the world wrote notes to himself at night.

Mostly about how to be a decent person the next morning.
---
# On mornings
Expect to meet difficult people today. Meddling, ungrateful, rude.

Then go out, and don't become one of them.

~ Book II
---
# On getting up
When you would rather stay under the blanket, tell yourself:

==I am getting up to do the work of a human being.==

~ Book V
---
# On getting even
The best revenge is not to become like the person who wronged you.

~ Book VI
---
# On time
Don't live as if you had ten thousand years.

While you are alive, while you can, be good.

~ Book IV
---
# On getting away
People look for a cottage by the sea or a house in the hills.

There is no quieter place to retreat to than your own mind, and it is always open.

~ Book IV
---
# On enough
==Very little is needed for a happy life.==

It is all within you, in the way you think.

~ Book VII
---
# On what upsets you
When something outside you causes you pain, it is mostly your judgement of it that hurts.

And your judgement, you can change.

~ Book VIII
---
# On letting go
Let things go the way a ripe olive falls:

thanking the tree that grew it.

~ Book IV
