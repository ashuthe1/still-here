@plant
# A plant that is somehow still alive.
You forget to water it. It forgives you.
---
@mug
# A mug that is yours.
Chipped, a bit ugly, and nobody else is allowed to use it.
---
@keys
# A lock with a trick to it.
Lift, push, turn. Only the people who live there know.
---
@rotis
# Something cooking.
The smell reaches the door before you do.
---
@night
# A window you look out of at night.
Same lights, same street. It calms you down.
---
@bed
# The right pillow.
You've tried others. They were wrong.
---
@door
# Someone saying "you're back".
Even if it's just you, saying it to the plant.
---
@laundry
# A pile of clothes on a chair.
Every home has one. It's practically the law.
