# Stand still on purpose.
I've been on one leg for an hour.

People think I'm doing nothing. I'm doing ==exactly this.==
---
# Let the water settle.
If you keep stirring it, you can't see a thing.

Leave it alone. It clears by itself.
---
# Most of the day is waiting.
The fish takes two seconds.

The rest is the job too.
---
# Look like you meant it.
I missed one this morning.

I stared at the sky as if that had been the plan.
---
# Don't chase everything that moves.
Most things that move are leaves.
---
# Find your bit of river.
Mine has a bent reed and an old shopping trolley.

I wouldn't swap it.
---
# Some days nothing comes.
Those days count too.

You turned up, you stood there, you went home.
---
# Fly when it's time.
Not before. Not much after.

You'll know.
