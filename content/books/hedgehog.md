# Spikes are allowed.
When something frightens me, I become a ball.

It isn't personal. That's what spikes are for.
---
# They go down when you feel safe.
Give me a minute and a quiet room.

You'd be surprised how soft I am.
---
# Not everyone needs to hold you.
Only a few people get to.

I choose them carefully. They wear gloves at first.
---
# Snuffle about.
Most of my day is poking my nose into things. Leaves, beetles, somebody's shoe.

Curiosity costs nothing.
---
# Go to bed early in winter.
From November I'm not available.

Leave a message.
---
# Be small and brave.
I crossed a whole road last night.

Very slowly. I looked both ways four times.
---
# Let one person see your soft side.
Underneath, I'm all soft.

That part is for the people I trust. ==Pick someone kind.==
---
# You're not too prickly.
You just haven't met everyone who doesn't mind yet.
