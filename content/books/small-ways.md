---
title: "Small ways people say I love you"
hook: "None of them use the words."
kind: list
motif: envelope
accent: "#e3807f"
order: 3
ask: "people/what-does-being-loved-well-feel-like-to"
close: "Notice one of these this week. Then do one."
---
@cake
# They save you the last piece.
And then say they're full.
---
@phone
# They send you a photo of something that reminded them of you.
A dog that looks like you. A shop with your name on it.
---
@glasses
# They remember the name of your difficult boss.
And on Thursday they ask how the meeting went.
---
@keys
# They say "message me when you reach".
And they mean it. They are waiting.
---
@umbrella
# They tilt the umbrella towards you.
One of their shoulders gets wet. They never mention it.
---
@chai
# They make your tea the way you like it.
Without asking any more.
---
@bed
# They let you sleep.
They tiptoe, they close the door, they take the call outside.
---
@shoebox
# They keep your old things.
A drawing, a ticket stub, a card you wrote when you were nine.
---
@night
# They notice when you go quiet.
And instead of asking what's wrong, they come and sit next to you.
