@envelope
My first salary came on a Friday.

I felt like a king.
---
@watch
I bought Papa a watch. Not an expensive one.

The man in the shop said it was "very elegant".
---
@watch
Papa looked at it for a long time.

> Why are you wasting money? Return it.
---
@watch
He wore it the next morning.

And the next.
---
@chairs
He wore it to every wedding that year.

He checked the time a lot. Especially near relatives.
---
@glasses
If anyone asked, he'd say, "Oh, this? My son bought it."

Very casually. About three times per wedding.
---
@shoebox
Years later, I found the box in his cupboard.

Inside was the receipt, folded small, and a note in his handwriting:
---
@note
# First salary.
==He bought me a watch.==
---
@watch
He still wears it. It loses two minutes a day.

> That's how I know it's the real one.
