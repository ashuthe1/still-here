# Hold on to what holds you up.
This tree has carried me since I was small.

I hold it back. That seems fair.
---
# Sleep twenty hours if you need to.
My food has almost no energy in it.

Some weeks are like that.
---
# Know what you like.
Leaves. Only leaves. Particular leaves, from particular trees.

I'm not difficult. I'm specific.
---
# Hug first.
When in doubt, I hug. Trees, branches, other koalas.

It has rarely been the wrong call.
---
# Go up when the ground gets loud.
Down there it's dogs and cars.

Up here it's wind and leaves.
---
# You were carried. Carry someone.
My mother carried me on her back for a year.

That's how it goes round.
---
# Change trees carefully.
Even I move sometimes.

Slowly, and always holding the next branch before I let go of this one.
---
# Look sleepy in every photo.
I always do.

People love me anyway.
