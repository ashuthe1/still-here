For my tenth birthday I wanted a new bicycle. Everyone in my class had one.

Papa said money was tight that year. I sulked for a week.
---
@door
On my birthday there was a blue bicycle by the front door.

A shiny bell. A ribbon on the handlebar.
---
@bicycle
It wasn't new. The paint was new.

When I turned it over, I could see old red under the seat.
---
@bicycle
< Papa, this is somebody's old bike.
> It's a good bike. It already knows the roads.
---
@sun
I rode it for four years. To school, to cricket, to nowhere in particular.

It never once broke down.
---
@cake
At Papa's sixtieth birthday, my uncle told the story.
---
@keys
That year, Papa had sold his scooter. He walked to the factory every day for a year.

Forty minutes each way.
---
@night
He bought the bicycle from a neighbour, and painted it in the garage at night.

Three coats, so it wouldn't chip.
---
@chai
I asked him about it later.

> It was a good scooter.

That's all he said.

~ He still walks everywhere. He says it's for his knees.
