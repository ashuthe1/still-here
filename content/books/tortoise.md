# Start before you feel ready.
I have never once felt ready.

I've been walking since Tuesday.
---
# Let people pass you.
A hare went past this morning.

Then again at lunch. Same hare.

I think he's lost.
---
# Take your home with you.
Wherever you stop, sit down. You're home.

Very convenient. Low rent.
---
# Go back inside for a while.
When the world gets loud, I pull my head in.

Nobody has ever complained. Nobody has noticed.
---
# Small steps count.
Yesterday I moved four metres.

==Personal best.==
---
# Lie in the sun.
I spent most of the afternoon on a warm stone.

It was the most useful thing I did all week.
---
# You'll get there.
I don't know where there is.

But I'm a bit closer than I was. That's how it works.
---
# Live a long time.
I'm ninety-one.

The secret is ==not rushing==, and lettuce.
