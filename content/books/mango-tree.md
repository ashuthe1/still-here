Rohan and I spent every summer of primary school in the mango tree behind his house.
---
@tree
We had rules.

No telling. No crying. Always share the last mango.

Then his family moved to another city.

We wrote letters for a year. Then texts. Then birthdays only.
---

Nobody fought. We just got busy.
