# Why he went.
==I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately.==

To see what life was, when you took away everything extra.
---
# On detail.
"Our life is frittered away by detail."

His advice was two words long: simplify, simplify.
---
# On being rich.
"A man is rich in proportion to the number of things which he can afford to let alone."

Wealth, measured in what you don't need.
---
# On mornings.
Every morning, he wrote, was "a cheerful invitation" to make his life simple again.

You get one of those tomorrow too.
---
# On your own pace.
If you don't keep pace with the people around you,

"perhaps it is because he hears a different drummer."
---
# On dreams.
"If you have built castles in the air, your work need not be lost; that is where they should be."

Now put the foundations under them.
---
# On time.
"Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in."

Not something to fight. Something to sit beside.
