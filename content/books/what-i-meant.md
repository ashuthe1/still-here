B: Ask me once more and I'll tell you.
---

---
A: "It's nothing, really."
B: I worked on it for weeks. Please notice.
---
A: "Drive safe."
B: You are the most important part of my day.
---
A: "Did you eat?"
B: I love you. We just never said it that way in our house.
---
A: "No, you have it."
B: I wanted it too. I wanted you to have it more.
---
A: "Text me when you're home."
B: I won't sleep until you do.
---
A: "Sorry, I'm being silly."
B: This matters to me, and I'm scared it doesn't matter to you.
---
A: "I'm just tired."
B: I've been tired for a while, and I don't know how to say it.
