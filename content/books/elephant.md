# Remember where the water was.
In a dry year, I still know where the river runs.

The good places stay in you.
---
# Remember who was kind.
Years ago, an old elephant let me stand in her shade.

I still walk her way.
---
# Forget some things on purpose.
The thorn I stepped on as a calf? Let it go.

I kept the walk, though. It was a lovely walk.
---
# Walk together.
We walk in a line, the little ones in the middle.

Nobody gets left at the back.
---
# Big doesn't have to be loud.
I can walk so softly you won't hear me.

Most strong things are quiet.
---
# Say hello with your whole face.
When I see someone I love, my ears flap and my trunk goes up.

No point hiding it.
---
# Roll in the mud.
Very good for the skin.

==Even better for the mood.==
---
# Keep the ones who stayed.
My oldest friends have grey on their faces now. So do I.

We still meet at the river.
