# Use what's around.
My nest is twigs, string, and half a bus ticket.

It's very comfortable.
---
# Make friends with the neighbours.
The tea stall man drops crumbs on purpose.

I know he does. He knows I know.
---
# Go where the people are.
Markets, rooftops, busy streets.

There's always a little something for a small bird.
---
# Sing, even if you're ordinary.
I'm not a nightingale. I'm brown.

I sing anyway. Mostly about breakfast.
---
# Have a bath in a puddle.
Splash, shake, done.

You'll feel like a new bird.
---
# Stay with your flock.
Alone, I worry about cats.

Together, we argue about seeds. ==Much better.==
---
# Small has its uses.
Nobody looks twice at a sparrow.

That's how I get the good crumbs.
---
# Wherever you land, start building.
Home is mostly the part where you decide to stay.
