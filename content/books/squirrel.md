# Save a little when you can.
One nut today, one tomorrow.

By winter it's quite a lot of nuts.
---
# Don't keep everything in one place.
I have stores all over the park.

If one goes, I don't.
---
# You'll forget some. That's fine.
Half my nuts I never find again.

They turn into trees. Not a bad mistake.
---
# Run, then stop completely.
I sprint, I freeze, I look around.

Very good for thinking.
---
# Fell off? Climb back up.
I fall off branches more than you'd think.

Nobody saw. Back up.
---
# Plans change.
I had a whole plan for that acorn.

A pigeon had a different plan.
---
# Enjoy the summer too.
Not every day is for storing.

Some days are for lying flat on a warm branch.
---
# Winter is easier when you've prepared.
Not easy. ==Easier.==

That's all I'm aiming for.
