The first message came on a Sunday at 7:40 in the evening, from a number I didn't know.

> Did you eat?
---
@phone
I ignored it. The next Sunday, same time:

> Did you eat? There's soup if you want to come by.
---
@phone
The third Sunday, I wrote back.

< Sorry, I think you have the wrong number.
---
@phone
Three dots. Then nothing. Then:

> Oh dear. My son changed his number and didn't tell me.
> That sounds like him.
---
@mug
I don't know why I sent the next part.

< For what it's worth, I did eat. Toast.
> Toast is not dinner.
---
@clock
After that, every Sunday at 7:40:

> Did you eat?

And I'd tell her. Pasta. Curry. Toast again, and a lecture.
---
@envelope
One month, her son called her. She told me before she told anyone.

> He's coming home. I'm making ==far too much food.==
---
@phone
I thought that was the end of it. The next Sunday, at 7:40:

> Did you eat?

~ She still asks. I still answer.
