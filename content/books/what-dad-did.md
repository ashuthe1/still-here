A: Checked your tyres before you drove back.
B: I can't come with you, so I'm checking what I can.
---
A: Left the porch light on, though you said you'd be late.
B: I'm awake until it's off.
---
A: "Call when you reach."
B: My phone stays face up on the table until you do.
---
A: Asked what you earn, then went quiet.
B: I'm doing sums to see if you're all right, without asking.
---
A: Fixed the hinge you never asked him to fix.
B: I don't know how to talk about your week. I know how to fix a door.
---
A: Gave you the best pieces of the mango.
B: Have the good part. I'll say I like the seed.
---
A: Fell asleep in front of the TV, waiting up.
B: I wasn't watching the news. I was listening for the gate.
---
A: Said "Hmm. Okay." when you told him your big plan.
B: I'm scared, and I'm proud, and I'll back you anyway.
---
A: Grumbled about how much your gift cost.
B: Nobody has bought me anything in years. I don't know what to do with my face.
