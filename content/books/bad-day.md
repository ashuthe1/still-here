@bed
# Lower the bar.
Today, a shower counts. So does a glass of water.
---
@chai
# Make something warm.
Tea, soup, a hot water bottle. Bodies like warm things on bad days.
---
@phone
# Tell one person.
You don't need to explain it all. "Bad day" is enough.
---
@keys
# Go outside for ten minutes.
Even just to the corner and back.
---
@book
# Read something you've read before.
Old favourites are restful. You already know it ends well.
---
@note
# Write down what went wrong.
Then close the notebook. It's on paper now, not in your head.
---
@night
# Go to bed early.
Most bad days look smaller after a night's sleep.
---
@sun
# Remember it's one day.
Tomorrow doesn't know about today yet.
