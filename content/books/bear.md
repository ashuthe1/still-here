# Fill up while there's plenty.
Berries, honey, a long lunch with people you like.

Store it up. You'll need it later.
---
# Find a cave.
Somewhere small and warm that is only yours.

It doesn't need to be impressive. Mine has a draught.
---
# Do less in a dark season.
In winter I do almost nothing.

Nobody calls it lazy. They call it winter.
---
# Sleep is not wasted time.
I sleep for months.

I wake up the same bear, only rested.
---
# Don't make big decisions half asleep.
I once tried to move caves in January.

Terrible idea. ==Wait for spring.==
---
# Spring always comes.
I've been through a lot of winters.

Every single one ended. Usually around March.
---
# Come out slowly.
First day out, just sit by the cave and feel the sun.

The river isn't going anywhere.
---
# Then go and find the honey.
You've earned it.

Actually, you didn't need to earn it. Just go.
