Sunday afternoon. Nani and I were folding laundry on her bed.

I asked her things I had never thought to ask.
---
@laundry
< Nani, how did you know Nana was the one?
> I didn't. I decided.
> Then I kept deciding, every morning, for fifty-one years.
---
@chai
< Weren't you ever scared?
> All the time.
> You don't wait for the fear to leave. It never leaves. You bring it along.
---
@chai
< What do you wish you'd done more of?
> Sat down.
> I was always standing up for something. Half my tea went cold.
---
@plant
< And less of?
> Worrying what the neighbours thought.
> Half of them moved to Canada. All that effort.
---
@bed
< What's the secret to a long marriage?
> Two blankets.
---
@glasses
< Do you miss being young?
> I miss my knees.
> Not the rest. Being young is a lot of work.
---
@laundry
< Will I be okay, Nani?

She stopped folding.

> I have watched you since you were this big.
> ==Yes.==
