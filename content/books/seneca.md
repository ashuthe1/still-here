# Life isn't short.
We aren't given a short life, he wrote.

We make it short by wasting so much of it.
---
# On guarding your time.
People guard their money and their land fiercely.

Then they hand their hours to anyone who asks for them.
---
# On later.
We plan for "later" as if later were promised.

Begin living now. The future isn't yours yet.
---
# On being busy.
Being busy is not the same as living.

The busiest people are the last to learn how.
---
# On grey hair.
Grey hair doesn't mean someone has lived long.

They may only have been around for a long time.
---
# On the past.
The past is the one part of your life no one can take from you.

Look back at it kindly.
---
# On real leisure.
The ones truly at leisure are those who make time to read, to think, to talk with wise people, living or long gone.
