@door
# Leave the party early.
Say goodnight to the host, find your coat, and go.

The party will carry on. That's what parties do.
---
@book
# Read the same book again.
You'll be a different person the second time.

So, in a way, it's a different book.
---
@note
# Change your mind.
You decided with what you knew then.

You know more now.
---
@phone
# Reply tomorrow.
The message will still be there in the morning.

So will you, and you'll be kinder.
---
@plant
# Be a beginner at forty.
Or at sixty, or eighty.

Everyone who is good at something was once bad at it in front of other people.
---
@mug
# Like what you like.
The song everyone is tired of. The film with the bad reviews.

==Toast for dinner.==
---
@bed
# Rest before you're exhausted.
You don't have to collapse first to deserve a sit-down.
---
@sun
# Take the long way home.
Especially on a good evening.
---
@night
# Not know yet.
What you want. Where you'll live. Who you're becoming.

"I don't know yet" is a whole answer.
