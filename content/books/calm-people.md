@clock
# They leave ten minutes early.
Most of their calm is simply not running.
---
@phone
# They don't reply instantly.
Not rude. Just not on call.
---
@mug
# They do one thing at a time.
Tea first. Then the email.
---
@plant
# They have something slow in their life.
A garden, a long walk, a pot of dal on a low flame.
---
@note
# They write things down.
Their head is for thinking, not for storing.
---
@door
# They leave when it's done.
The party, the argument, the office.
---
@glasses
# They ask before assuming.
"What did you mean by that?" saves a lot of sleepless nights.
---
@bed
# They protect their sleep.
Everything is harder when you're tired, and they know it.
