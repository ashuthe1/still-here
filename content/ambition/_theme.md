Nobody who built something that lasted was in a hurry. They just kept turning up at the same desk, in the same bad mood, for years.

This room is about that. Craft, nerve, and long games with people who stay. There is no grind in here. Mostly there is patience, and one man who got embarrassingly excited about a calligraphy class.
