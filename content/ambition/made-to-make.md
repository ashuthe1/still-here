You have had a week of only taking things in. The feed, the shows, the takes, the three-hour podcast about something you do not care about. And now there is a flat, grey feeling you cannot quite name.

Here is a name for it: you have been eating all week and never once cooked.

People are built to make things. Not as a brand, not for an audience. As a need, like sleep. Skip it for long enough and something in you goes quiet in a way that no amount of more content fixes.

Make something today. It can be terrible. A paragraph. A sketch. A fix to the thing in your kitchen that has annoyed you for a year. The size does not matter. The direction does. Check how you feel by evening.
