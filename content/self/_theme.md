At some point you were handed a role. The responsible one. The quiet one. The one who is fine. You played it so well you forgot it was a part.

This room is for the person underneath. The one who feels everything and shows almost none of it, who never quite fit, who learned early to take up less space. There was never anything wrong with you. The room was too small.
