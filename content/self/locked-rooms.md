A nineteen-year-old cadet at a military academy wrote to Rilke asking whether he should be a poet, and whether his poems were any good. Rilke wrote back and refused to answer either question. He said the asking was the problem.

You do not figure out who you are. You live it, mostly without noticing, and one morning you realise you walked into the answer years ago without being told.

The locked rooms are not keeping you out. They are keeping something for later. Stop rattling the handles.
