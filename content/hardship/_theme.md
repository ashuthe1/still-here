Nobody picks the storm. You are here because it found you, or because it has not let go yet.

There is no advice in this room. Just notes from people who were in it too, scratched on the wall for whoever came next. Some of them were in prison camps. Some of them were in a bedroom with the curtains shut for a month. Both count. Read them slowly.
