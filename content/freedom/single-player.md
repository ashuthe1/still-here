tags: [independence]
---
It sounds cold until you sit with it a while.

Nobody else is in your head. Nobody else will be in the bed at the end. Every performance you keep up, every opinion you are managing, is for an audience that is not actually watching, and would not be there if it were.

And then the strange part. Once you accept the game is single player, you get kinder to everyone else. Because you stop needing anything from them.
