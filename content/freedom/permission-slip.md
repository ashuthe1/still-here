You have been standing at a counter for years, waiting for someone to come out of the back and give you the go-ahead. Start the thing. Leave the job. Call yourself a writer, or whatever the word is.

There is nobody in the back. There never was. The counter is something you built so you would have somewhere to wait.

Walk around it.
